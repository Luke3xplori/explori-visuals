Chapter 2 · A different philosophy

From selling space to curating outcomes

The hall plan

Trade show: sell the space

  • Revenue mainly from the sale of exhibit space
  • Success read in square metres and footfall
  • Exhibitors engage mostly from their booth

ConEx: curate the room

  • Revenue mainly from sponsorship packages and bespoke activations
  • Success read in who is in the room
  • Curated meetings, hosted buyers and off-booth activations
“Instead we think about relationships and who needs to be in that room and what they do once they’re in there.”Jay Weintraub, Founder and CEO, Connectiv
